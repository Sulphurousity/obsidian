*Power Rule*:
If:
$$ \large \color{lightblue} y=ax^n$$
Then:
$$ \large \color{lightblue} y=n*ax^{n-1}$$

*Product Rule*:
If:
$$ \large \color{lightblue} y=f(x)*g(x)$$
Then:
$$ \large \color{lightblue} y'=f(x)*g'(x)+g(x)*f'(x)$$
Evaluate:
$$ \large \color{lightblue} y=(x^{-2}+3)(3x^3-x)$$
$$ \large \color{lightblue} {dy\over dx}=(x^{-2}+3)(9x^2-1)+(-2x^{-3})(3x^3-x)$$

*Quotient Rule*:
If:
$$ \large \color{lightblue} y={f(x)\over g(x)}$$
Then:
$$ \large \color{lightblue} {dy\over dx}={g(x)*f'(x)-f(x)*g'(x)\over (g(x))^2}$$

*Chain Rule*:
If:
$$ \large \color{lightblue} y=f(g(x))$$
Then:
$$ \large \color{lightblue} y=f'(g(x))\cdot g'(x)$$

*sin*:
$$\large \color{lightblue} \frac{d}{dx}[\sin x]=\cos x$$
$$\large \color{lightblue} \frac{d}{dx}[\cos x]=-\sin x$$
*tan*:
$$\large \color{lightblue} \frac{d}{dx}[\tan x]=\sec^2x$$
$$\large \color{lightblue} \frac{d}{dx}[\cot x]-\csc^2x$$
*sec*:
$$\large \color{lightblue} \frac{d}{dx}[\sec x]=\sec x\tan x$$
$$\large \color{lightblue} \frac{d}{dx}[\csc x]=-\csc x\cot x$$
