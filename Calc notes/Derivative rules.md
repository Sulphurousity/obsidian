**Power Rule**:
If:
$$y=ax^n$$
Then:
$$y=n*ax^{n-1}$$

**Product Rule**:
If:
$$y=f(x)*g(x)$$
Then:
$$y'=f(x)*g'(x)+g(x)*f'(x)$$
Evaluate:
$$y=(x^{-2}+3)(3x^3-x)$$

**Quotient Rule**:
If:
$$y={f(x)\over g(x)}$$
Then:
$${dy\over dx}={g(x)*f'(x)-f(x)*g'(x)\over (g(x))^2}$$

**Chain Rule**:
If:
$$y=f(g(x))$$
Then:
$$y=f'(g(x))\cdot g'(x)$$
