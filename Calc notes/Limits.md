$$\lim\limits_{x \to 1}(\frac{2e^x+1}{e^x})$$

Factor and reduce:
$$\lim\limits_{x\to 3}\frac{x^4+x^3}{2x^3}$$

Expand:
$$\lim\limits_{h\to 0}\frac{(5+h)^2-25}{h}$$

Simplify the complex fraction:
$$\lim\limits_{t\to 0}\frac{4-\frac1{t^2}}{3+\frac2t+\frac1{t^2}}$$
multiply by the smallest denominator:
$$\lim\limits_{t\to 0}\frac{4t^2-1}{3t^2+2t+1}=\frac{-1}{1}=-1$$

Multiply by the conjugate:
$$\lim\limits_{x\to 16}\frac{x-16}{\sqrt{x}-4}*\frac{\sqrt{x}+9}{\sqrt{x}+9}$$
$$\lim\limits_{x\to 16}\frac{(x-16)(\sqrt{x}+4)}{(x-16)}=\sqrt{x}+4$$

$\frac{sinx}x$ limit identity:
$$\lim\limits_{x\to0}\frac{sinx}{x}=1$$
$$\lim\limits_{x\to0}\frac{3sinx}{2x}=\lim\limits_{x\to0}\frac32*\lim\limits_{x\to0}\frac{sinx}{x}$$
$$\frac32*1=\frac32$$
